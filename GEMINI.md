# Project Rules

- **Icon Style**: Never use colorful emojis for UI icons or graphics. Use clean, flat SVG-style icons (inline SVG or SVG symbols) across all components.

## Natural Writing & Anti-AI Tone Guidelines

To keep all website copy, headings, and descriptions authentic, grounded, and human (preventing detectable AI patterns):

1. **Ban AI Buzzwords**: Never use terms like *delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, beacon, crucial, paramount, multifaceted, seamless, boasts, nestled, poised to, vibrant, furthermore, moreover, additionally*.
2. **No Negative Parallelisms**: Avoid formulaic structures like *"not only... but also"*, *"not just X, but Y"*, or *"rather than"*. State facts directly.
3. **Avoid Rule-of-Three Triads**: Do not force adjectives or features into repetitive triplets (e.g. *"clean, bright, and convenient"*).
4. **Use Direct Verbs**: Use *"is"*, *"has"*, *"provides"* instead of *"serves as"*, *"stands as a testament to"*, or *"functions as"*.
5. **No Puffery or Generic Hype**: Stick to concrete local details, exact machine capacities (20lb to 80lb), real hours (7am–9pm), clear pricing ($1.50–$2.00/lb), and specific locations (3330 W Laskey Rd).
6. **Natural Punctuation**: Do not overuse em dashes (`—`) or unnecessary bolding across entire blocks of text.
